Ward round coordination

Where the ward's walking time goes, how often patients who need more than one specialty are seen by them together, and what would change if rounds were planned around each other.

About this page
No real data
No patient, clinician or ward identifier from any real record appears anywhere on this page. Every figure is computed from synthetic demo data by the same code that would run against a real extract, so the shapes and the caveats are real even though the numbers describe nobody. ADR-0005.
Why there are five definitions
Whether a patient "needed" a specialty is not recorded anywhere directly — it has to be inferred, and referrals, consult notes and problem lists disagree. Rather than pick one and present it as the answer, all five are computed and the selector switches between them. The distance between the widest and the strictest is the honest uncertainty in the question itself, and it is usually larger than the confidence interval on any single one. SPEC-001.
What this page will not do
It will not rank schedules or recommend one, it will not break any figure down by individual clinician, and it will not show a group small enough to identify someone. Each of those is a decision on record rather than a feature that has not been built yet. ADR-0004, ADR-0006 rule 5, ADR-0005.
Full method
Each figure carries its own "How this was measured" panel, naming the estimator, the interval and the decision that governs how it may be reported.
